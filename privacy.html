Privacy Policy

Wanted Level Uploader. Last updated November 10, 2026.

What this app is

Wanted Level Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Wanted Level YouTube channel. It uploads videos to that channel and reads that channel's own analytics. It has no public sign-up and no other users.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel, with their titles, descriptions, and tags.
  • Read-only information about the authorized channel and its videos.
  • Read-only YouTube Analytics reports for the authorized channel, such as views and watch time.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to review how they perform.
  • The authorization token and any retrieved analytics are stored on the channel owner's own computer.
  • Analytics data retrieved through the API is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app does not collect data from viewers or from any other YouTube user.
  • The app does not use cookies or tracking on this site.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access removes the app's ability to reach the account. Stored tokens and data can be deleted on request using the contact below.
